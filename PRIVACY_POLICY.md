# Brawl Hub Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-16  
**Operator:** BrawlHub  
**Contact:** brawlhub@gmail.com  

This Privacy Policy explains how Brawl Hub Bot (the “Bot”) handles data when it is installed on a Discord server. The Bot provides private support tickets, staff controls, ticket transcripts, inactivity reminders, and ticket administration.

## Data the Bot receives

Depending on the features enabled by a server administrator, the Bot may receive and store:

- Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, and related Discord metadata;
- usernames or display names shown in ticket transcripts;
- ticket subjects, form answers, ticket status, assignments, priorities, timestamps, and administrator actions;
- internal staff notes added to tickets;
- message text, timestamps, authors, and attachment links from ticket-channel transcripts;
- technical error and audit information needed to operate and secure the Bot.

The Bot does not use Discord data for advertising, profiling, selling data, or training machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to:

- create and operate support tickets;
- control access to private ticket and staff-control channels;
- record ticket activity and provide inactivity reminders;
- create transcripts when tickets are closed or deleted;
- provide ticket history, administration, auditing, and troubleshooting.

The Bot processes message content only in ticket channels that it can access. It does not intentionally save message content from unrelated public channels as ticket transcripts.

## Where data is stored

Ticket data is stored in the database configured by the Bot operator. The current deployment uses a Turso/libSQL database. A deployment may instead use a local SQLite database on the Bot's server. Discord data is also processed by Discord according to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

The operator does not sell or share Bot data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the Bot and its database, or disclosed when required by law.

## Retention

Ticket transcripts are retained for the number of days configured by the server administrator and are then automatically deleted when expiration cleanup runs. Ticket records, configuration, administrator records, and audit records may be retained for as long as needed to operate the Bot, investigate abuse, maintain accountability, or comply with legal obligations.

Server administrators may delete tickets and their associated Discord channels through the Bot. A deletion request may also be submitted to the operator using the contact address above. The operator will review and delete data that is no longer required, subject to legal or security obligations.

## User choices and rights

Users can stop using the Bot by not creating or participating in tickets, by leaving the relevant server, or by asking the server administrator to remove the Bot. Users may contact the operator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held by the Bot. The operator may need to verify the request and coordinate with the relevant server administrator.

## Security

The operator uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Bot data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Bot tokens, database credentials, and authentication tokens must be kept private and must not be published in source code or screenshots.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features, storage, or legal obligations change. The current version will be published at the policy URL shown in the Discord Developer Portal.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **brawlhub@gmail.com** and include the Discord server name, relevant ticket ID or channel ID, and the request you want handled.
